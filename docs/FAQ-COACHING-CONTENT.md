# Coaching FAQ content — to publish in Batch 27I

## How is the coaching score calculated?

60% of the score is board completion. 40% is the share of provable decisions that were good. Move 0 and uncertain choices are not graded as decisions. If no later decision can be proved, no score is shown. This is a learning aid, not an official rank.

## How are moves assessed?

Move 0 opens the board and is not assessed. Uncertain moves are not automatically mistakes or unavoidable guesses.
