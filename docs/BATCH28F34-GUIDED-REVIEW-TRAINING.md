# Batch 28F.3–4 — Guided training and practice from completed reviews

Baseline: commit a3ffbb1 plus the verified, uncommitted 28F.2 starter training, controls and header patches. Keep the complete 28F.2 changes with this batch when staging.

Starter and review-derived exercises use the same exact-layout preparation and answer checker. The board is static, and the explanation refers to the selected square. Incorrect choices explain whether that square is provably safe, mined or genuinely uncertain; compatible-layout uncertainty is not a claim of 50/50 odds. Three progressively disclosed hints show the starting clue, its remaining-mine constraint and a full conclusion with the existing solver's traced dependency steps when available. Missing or cyclic derivations fall back to the exact-layout conclusion. The actual accepted alternative square is explained after a correct answer. Hints persist on retry, reset on navigation, and do not award a gameplay result. Clue, related-square and final-answer highlights have text labels, including an explanation that the final outline does not imply safety. Existing desktop, touch and keyboard controls are unchanged.

Completed review screen: `Train from your mistakes` appears only when the already prepared, post-opening review has a verified mistake. `Practice my mistakes` explicitly prepares at most three exercises from at most four distinct candidate moves. The helper receives only public dimensions, total mines and the prepared replay frames, and extracts the complete visible **before** board. It does not consume the actual mine layout, after-board or old proof decisions for grading. Full boards up to 480 cells and 30 per side are permitted (including standard Beginner, Intermediate and Expert). The same independent exact solver must finish; inconsistent, limited, terminal and unsupported positions are skipped. Mistaken flag and direct known-mine reveal become classification exercises; unnecessary guesses can become guaranteed-safe alternative exercises if supported. Known-mine chord mistakes are not currently converted. A direct reveal that exact analysis establishes as safe is not taught as an unnecessary guess. No crop, hidden-layout fallback, inferred certainty or automatic replay is used. Review training is scoped to the open completed review and unmounts when the existing review/account boundary clears selection. The panel does not fetch, save or award anything. Full boards scroll horizontally as needed; do not shrink the complete board to a misleading crop.

No SQL, server API, dependencies, paid AI, XP, Elo, game history or grading changes. Training attempts remain temporary; adaptive selection and persistence are deferred. The candidate/solver limits mean some games will produce no eligible puzzle, including difficult positions, or reviews without mistakes. Difficulty labels indicate board size for review-derived exercises, not measured skill.

Verification: focused `node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/batch28f*.test.mjs`, full `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, `git diff --check`; full browser application verification on developer PC. Confirm wrong/correct feedback, hints and reset, personalized completed review puzzle and no-eligible state where applicable, full-board scrolling and account sign-out. No deployment.

Coordinate accessibility: both training entry points share visible one-based row and column numbers in the same scrollable grid. All feedback, hints and displayed solver steps expand R/C notation to plain row and column wording, without altering proof evidence or answer indices.
